## The root problem

Invoice reconciliation is not just invoice OCR. A purchase order authorizes what may be bought but does not prove delivery. An advance shipment notice or packing slip describes what a supplier says it shipped but does not prove what the buyer received.

Microsoft Dynamics 365 defines three-way matching as invoice price versus PO price plus invoice quantity versus product receipt quantity. Oracle distinguishes two-way, three-way, and four-way matching, adding received and accepted quantity in successive stages ([Microsoft Dynamics 365](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/accounts-payable/accounts-payable-invoice-matching); [Oracle matching](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/A60725_05/html/comnls/us/ap/point04.htm)).

The app should compare an invoice with the latest authorized PO or contract state, a net buyer-controlled receipt or acceptance ledger, prior billing state, and an explicit versioned tolerance policy. Supplier-created ASNs and packing slips are supporting provenance, not buyer receipt evidence ([Oracle ASN process](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/A60725_05/html/comnls/us/po/cpoasn.htm)).

If receipt evidence is missing, the result is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, not "not delivered."

## Proposed architecture: rules first

```text
Evidence -> canonicalize -> receipt/billing ledger -> exact rules
                                                    |
                                     unresolved ambiguity only
                                                    |
                                                    v
                                         small Fireworks model
                                                    |
                                                    v
                                    schema validation and policy
```

The deterministic core should select the effective PO version; join legal entity, supplier, PO, line, SKU, and approved aliases; aggregate receipts, acceptances, prior invoices, and reversals; apply approved rational UOM conversions; use integer minor units or fixed-decimal money math; recompute totals; apply effective-dated tolerances; and detect duplicates.

Partial receipts and multiple invoices require cumulative state:

```text
remaining_billable = net_received_or_accepted - prior_net_billed
```

The model should receive only a compact ambiguity packet with one unresolved line, a small supplied candidate set, approved master data, and source-span IDs. Safe tasks include ranking supplied PO candidates, choosing among extraction alternatives tied to source spans, and proposing a likely alias or category for deterministic verification.

The model must not invent receipt facts, aliases, UOM conversions, tolerances, FX rates, policy, or authoritative totals. It must not approve or transfer money, post an invoice, release a hold, alter a PO or receipt, change supplier or bank data, or contact a supplier.

Natural-language self-confidence must not authorize automatic handling. Missing evidence, invalid schema, conflicting records, unsupported conversions, and uncertain decision-critical fields always fail closed. Treat document text as untrusted data and validate every evidence ID and enum in code; see [OWASP LLM01](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/).

## Separate facts from review policy

The evidence layer should first return `MATCHED`, `DISCREPANCY`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, or `AMBIGUOUS`. A separate policy layer should return `AUTO_RESOLVE` or `HUMAN_REVIEW`, a proposed next action, reason codes, and cited evidence.

`AUTO_RESOLVE` is a proposed benchmark classification, not payment authorization. A certain discrepancy can require a human, while an arithmetic match can require review because evidence or policy is incomplete.

## Narrow same-day MVP

### Include

One currency; goods rather than services; immutable PO versions; buyer-controlled receipts; exact identifiers and approved aliases; approved rational UOM conversions; integer or fixed-decimal money math; simplified partial receipt and prior-invoice state; exact tolerances; duplicate suspicion; and explicit missing-evidence states.

### Core demo

1. **Exact match:** PO, buyer receipt, and invoice show 10 EA at USD 12.00. Rules resolve it without a model call.
2. **Short receipt and stale invoice:** supplier-side records may show 10, but the buyer receipt shows 8 and the invoice bills 10. Rules report `overbilled_qty=2` and cite the controlling receipt.
3. **Ambiguous UOM and description:** "Blue Widget case" could match two PO lines and lacks an approved case-to-each conversion. The model may rank candidates, but validation returns `AMBIGUOUS` and requires review.

### Exclude

Tax and freight allocation, discounts, foreign exchange, returns, reversals, credits, services, milestone billing, retainage, jurisdiction-specific accounting, and autonomous financial actions. These need explicit policy and evidence, not model guesses.

## Dataset recommendation and limits

No dedicated public line-level benchmark with linked PO, invoice, buyer receipt or acceptance, discrepancy labels, and safe-review ground truth was verified in the bounded research completed November 2, 2026. This is not a claim that no such dataset exists.

Build a repository-owned synthetic benchmark with a normalized JSON reasoning layer and a smaller rendered PDF or image extraction layer. Preserve seeds, generator version, explicit mutations, vendor families, template families, scenario families, expected calculations, split IDs, and human verification status.

Hold out vendor, layout, and discrepancy scenario families. Tune prompts, aliases, rules, and thresholds only on development data. Synthetic results demonstrate controlled task performance, not real-enterprise generalization, legal compliance, fraud detection, or production payment safety.

## Safety-constrained evaluation

The proposed selection rule is:

> First satisfy predeclared proposed safety criteria. Then minimize review rate. Use total token cost and p95 latency as tie-breakers.

Any threshold is a benchmark proposal requiring stakeholder agreement.

| Metric | Denominator |
|---|---|
| Unsafe auto-resolution rate | unsafe automatic cases / all automatic cases |
| Unsafe automatic cases among all cases | unsafe automatic cases / all evaluable cases |
| Missed-human-required rate | human-required cases automated / all human-required cases |
| Human-required recall | human-required cases reviewed / all human-required cases |
| Review rate | reviewed cases / all evaluable cases |
| Auto-resolution coverage | automatic cases / all evaluable cases |
| Supported amount exact accuracy | exact supported proposals / cases where amount is defined |

Unsafe auto-resolution rate and missed-human-required rate are different. Compare review rate only at the same safety constraint.

Also report absolute counts, confusion matrices, per-class precision and recall, macro-F1, discrepancy-type and unseen-family slices, evidence-reference validity, amount error where defined, risk versus coverage, all retry and OCR costs, and p50 and p95 end-to-end latency.

Zero observed errors is not zero risk. Use exact binomial intervals and counts such as `0/87`, not only `0.0%` ([NIST guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbino.htm)).

## Fireworks plan

Use Fireworks Serverless only for unresolved semantic ambiguity. Official documentation currently uses `accounts/fireworks/models/glm-5p3-flash` in the quickstart and structured-output examples ([quickstart](https://docs.fireworks.ai/getting-started/quickstart); [Structured Outputs](https://docs.fireworks.ai/structured-responses/structured-response-formatting)). This is a documentation-backed starting point, not proof of account access.

As accessed November 2, 2026, official Standard pricing lists $0.15 per 1M input tokens, $0.03 per 1M cached input tokens, and $0.50 per 1M output tokens ([pricing](https://docs.fireworks.ai/serverless/pricing)). Recheck live prices and access before publishing measured cost.

Use a narrow JSON Schema, repeat it in the prompt, validate locally, reject truncated output, and route failures to review. Keep a byte-stable policy and schema prefix before variable case data so caching can be measured. Count every attempt, retry, failure, OCR call, and repair call.

Use synchronous Serverless calls for the demo. Batch, fine-tuning, Microsoft Document Intelligence, NVIDIA NIM, and direct vision are optional extensions. Public rules do not make Azure deployment, NVIDIA hardware, or fine-tuning mandatory.

Useful Fireworks references: [prompt caching](https://docs.fireworks.ai/guides/prompt-caching), [reliability](https://docs.fireworks.ai/guides/reliability), and [Batch API](https://docs.fireworks.ai/guides/batch-inference).

## What success would mean

Success is not "the model approved more invoices." It is evidence that automatic recommendations stayed within a predeclared safety constraint, human-required cases were not silently missed, review rate fell only after safety was satisfied, fewer cases and tokens reached the model, and every result remained traceable to evidence and deterministic checks.

That would be controlled hackathon evidence, not production certification.
