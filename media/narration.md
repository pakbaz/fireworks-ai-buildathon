# Demo video narration
Explainer video, about 1 min 53 s, narrated with the macOS Samantha voice.

**Video:** [watch on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cEZZ00eSJBZlKE0aDoeYgAf1HcPLOe6y/view?usp=sharing)

1. **Three papers.** Before a company pays a bill, three papers have to agree. The order, the delivery slip, and the invoice.
2. **They agree.** Same item, same count, same price. When they line up, the bill pays itself.
3. **Maria's morning.** Maria runs accounts payable. About one in five of her hundred daily invoices gets flagged for a person to check, at roughly ten dollars each.
4. **Mostly nothing.** So she opens them. Twelve units, written as one case. A count written by hand. An order number in a different format. All fine. Only the last one is a real problem.
5. **Can't read it.** The software couldn't read them. And guessing with money is unsafe, so it hands them to a person.
6. **Frontier vs us.** A frontier model reads the papers and decides in one pass, doing the math in its head. We split the job. A model twenty times cheaper only reads, and says when it can't. Plain code checks every number, using what the company knows.
7. **What the company knows.** That knowledge is concrete. A case of I V tubing holds fifty. This supplier writes the count by hand. A pump costs forty thousand dollars, a phone case three. Without the first fact, our system asks a person. With it, it pays.
8. **Three answers.** Every invoice gets one of three answers. Pay. Wait for the delivery. Or ask a person.
9. **The business dial.** How careful to be is the business's call. Two pumps in doubt at a hospital: ask a person. Two phone cases: cheaper to pay than to review. The default comes from what the system knows about the company.
10. **How we test it.** To prove it, we run a thousand invoices with known answers through Kimi K three alone, and through GLM five point three Flash: alone, in our harness, and with the company wiki. Did it catch every real problem? How many fine invoices still went to a person? And what did it cost?
11. **The goal.** The goal: Maria only sees the invoices that are actually wrong.
