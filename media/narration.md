# Demo video narration
Explainer video, 1 min 56 s, narrated with an ElevenLabs voice (Maya). The three-bucket slide sits at the exact middle.

**Video:** [media/demo-video.mp4](demo-video.mp4)

1. **Three papers.** Before a company pays a bill, three papers have to agree. The order, the delivery slip, and the invoice.
2. **They agree.** Same item, same count, same price. When they line up, the bill pays itself.
3. **Maria's morning.** Maria runs accounts payable. About one in five of her hundred daily invoices gets flagged.
4. **Mostly nothing.** So she opens them. Twelve units, written as one case. A count written by hand. An order number in a different format. All fine. Only the last one is a real problem.
5. **Can't read it.** The software couldn't read them. And guessing with money is unsafe, so it hands them to a person.
6. **Frontier vs us.** A frontier model reads the papers and decides in one pass. We split the job. A model twenty times cheaper only reads, and says when it can't. Plain code checks every number, using what the company knows.
7. **What the company knows.** A case of I V tubing holds fifty. A pump costs forty thousand dollars, a phone case three. Without the first fact, our system asks a person. With it, it pays.
8. **Three answers (the middle of the video).** Every invoice gets one of three answers. Pay. Wait for the delivery. Or ask a person.
9. **The business dial.** How careful to be is the business's call. Two pumps in doubt at a hospital: ask a person. Two phone cases: cheaper to pay than to review.
10. **The hard test.** The hard test: two hundred ten invoices where every document agrees. In one hundred forty, something is wrong compared with the company's history.
11. **Scoreboard.** A frontier model, told exactly what to check, caught a hundred twenty-one of a hundred forty, with six false alarms. Ours caught a hundred thirty-nine, with none, at a nineteenth of the cost.
12. **Why.** Why? Without company knowledge, we catch forty. With it, a hundred thirty-nine. So a cheap model is enough.
13. **The trade-off.** On ordinary invoices, both catch almost everything, and ours is twelve times cheaper. The trade-off: we send twenty-seven of five hundred fine invoices to a person.
14. **One invoice.** One invoice: eighty-two infusion pumps. This hospital has never ordered more than eleven. The frontier model says pay. Ours asks a person.
